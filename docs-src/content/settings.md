## Look and feel

Choose **light**, **dark** or **system**, and dock the panel left or right, or let it float. Under **Advanced**: **Request count on icon** turns the number on the toolbar icon on or off, and **Forget recorded clicks** clears which button made which call on this site.

## Shortcut

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> shows and hides the panel. Change it with **Change ↗** (Chrome’s shortcut page).

## Import & export

**Export…** saves your environments and users, rules, mocks, API docs and pinned endpoints as one JSON file. **Import…** brings them back here or in another browser, and can be undone.

> **Careful:** tokens are in the file when **Include tokens** is on. Share it only with people who should have them.
